1. Introduction

Modern retrievallaugmented generation (RAG) systems depend heavily on reliable document
parsing. However, PDFs often contain complex layouts such as multilicolumn text, tables,
mathematical expressions, and captions. Incorrect ordering during extraction can lead to corrupted
semantic chunks.
