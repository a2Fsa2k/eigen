## Abstract —  This mock document contains common formatting structures encountered in research
papers and technical documents. It includes headings, lists, tables, pseudo two I column layouts,
footnotes, code blocks, captions, and mixed text styles. The goal is to evaluate how well document
parsers and OCR pipelines preserve logical reading order and structure for downstream RAG
pipelines.
## 1. Introduction
Modern retrieval I augmented generation (RAG) systems depend heavily on reliable document
parsing. However, PDFs often contain complex layouts such as multi I column text, tables,
mathematical expressions, and captions. Incorrect ordering during extraction can lead to corrupted
semantic chunks.
## 2. Background
PDFs are primarily designed for visual fidelity rather
than semantic structure. Libraries often rely on
positional heuristics to infer reading order.
Challenges include:
1
Multi I column layouts
2
Figures with captions
3
Tables with merged cells
4
Footnotes and references
